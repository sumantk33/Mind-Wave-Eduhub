About Us

Who Are We?

Mindwave Edu Hub is a revolutionary platform dedicated to empowering educators and students through innovative training, meaningful resources, and hands-on learning experiences. We believe that true education emerges when scholarship meets empathy.

Our Vision

To empower 21st-century educators and learners through innovative training rooted in mindset shifts and technology integration, fostering student-centered, inclusive, and creative learning environments.

Our Mission

To equip teachers with modern pedagogical skills, emotional intelligence, and technological tools that inspire curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning.

The Need

Education today demands a paradigm shift from traditional methods to engaging, personalized, and experiential learning. Mind-Wave addresses this need by supporting educators holistically—professionally, emotionally, and intellectually.

Programs

Programs

We announce two flagship programs aimed at improvising teachers pedagogy that will enable them to guide students through experiential learning

3–4 Months

The Aspiring Teacher (TAT)

3-4 Months Training course for graduates to hone their teaching skills and create better job opportunities.

Flexible / Part-time

The Elevated Teacher (TET)

Course aimed at empowering in science teachers wanting to upgrade their skills

One Day

Student Science Camps (SSC)

One day science camps aimed at developing curiosity for 13-18 years students with hands on experience and interaction with reputed scientists and engineers working at the forefronts of science and technology.

Auxiliary programs

Short Awareness Programs

Other short awareness programs for school management, parents and other stake-holders.
